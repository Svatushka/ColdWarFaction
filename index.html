What is this site for?

This site is a community hub dedicated to factions in the Roblox place Cold War. It gathers information about factions, their recruitment, faction wars and tournaments in one place. Here you can find a faction to join or recruit new members, read beginner-friendly guides, follow patch notes and updates, and connect with other faction leaders and players. Our goal is to help factions grow, organize matches faster, and make the Cold War faction scene more active and visible.
